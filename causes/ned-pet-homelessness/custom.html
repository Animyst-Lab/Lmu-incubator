These numbers are estimates. They use the Amanda Foundation's figure that one unspayed female dog can have up to 12 puppies a year, multiplied by the number of dogs and years. Puppies can start having litters of their own by 6 months old, which isn't counted here, so the real ripple can be even bigger.

We don't show a dollar cost per surgery. The Spaymobile is free for pet owners, and the Amanda Foundation doesn't publish what one surgery costs, so we didn't want to guess what your donation pays for. Every dollar goes to their medical care, spay/neuter procedures, and education programs.
