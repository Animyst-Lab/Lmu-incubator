From one neighborhood to the world

Homeboy Industries grew from a small effort in East Los Angeles into a model for others around the world.

  1. 1988

    Began as a way of improving the lives of former gang members in East Los Angeles.

  2. 18 months

    The length of the program, with services like tattoo removal, mental health support and GED prep.

  3. 250+

    Organizations and social enterprises around the world that use Homeboy as a blueprint.

Find your way to help

Homeboy has three ways to volunteer. Pick the one that fits you.

Event-based volunteer

Help at a big event

A one-day commitment, mostly on Saturdays, supporting Homeboy's annual events and fundraisers, like its 5K, the Lo Maximo fundraiser and holiday events.

Specialized volunteer

Share what you know

1 to 4 hours on a weekday between 9am and 3:30pm, at least once a month, for at least a year. Tutors in math, language arts and computer basics are needed to help trainees prepare for the GED.

Volunteer advocate

Spread the word

Get involved on your own timing: learn about Homeboy's work and be a messenger for it in your community.
