    Turn hope into help

    Throw an Angel Party

    An Angel Party is a get-together that raises money for Breast Cancer Angels. It can be whatever you'd like it to be. Pick an idea to plan yours.

    1. Pick a date and give Breast Cancer Angels a call. They're happy to support you, and can even come speak about their work.
    2. Plan it your way, and ask a friend or two to help. Simple or an extravaganza, it's up to you.
    3. Invite friends and family, and share why it matters. Wear pink if you like!

    Ideas and tips from Breast Cancer Angels' Get Involved page. Use the volunteer and donate buttons on this page to reach them.
