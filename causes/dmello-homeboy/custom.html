An LMU story of second chances

Father Greg Boyle, who founded Homeboy Industries, is an LMU alum and on our Entrepreneurship Wall of Honor.

Homeboy shows what entrepreneurship can do for a community. It runs real businesses where people leaving gangs and prison get paid job training, and in its own words, it is “in the business of second chances.”

“We imagine a world without prisons, and then we try to create that world.” Father Greg Boyle, founder of Homeboy Industries
Homeboy's social enterprises

It all started with one bakery

Homeboy began as a single bakery in Los Angeles. Today its businesses cook, grow, print, recycle and groom dogs, and every one is a training ground for a second chance. Tap any business to learn more.

Source: homeboyindustries.org and Homeboy's 2024 Annual Report.
