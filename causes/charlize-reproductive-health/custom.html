    At every age

    Most STIs have no symptoms

    Most people with STIs never notice symptoms, but infections can still cause long-term health problems. If you're sexually active, testing about once a year, or before or after a new partner, is the way to know.

    Get care in LA

    Planned Parenthood Los Angeles

    Health centers across LA County offer birth control, STI testing and treatment, cancer screenings, and more, with judgment-free care. Find one at plannedparenthood.org.
